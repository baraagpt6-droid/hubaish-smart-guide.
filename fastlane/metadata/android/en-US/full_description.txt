Explore Hubaish District in Ibb Governorate through its geography, history, heritage sites, learning centres and notable people.

Guide content
• Administrative profile listing an area of 228 km².
• Population by year: 105,998 in the 2004 census and a 2025 estimate of 162,023, including residents and internally displaced people.
• The district's 22 sub-districts, separate from related historical regions.
• Heritage records for Wuhaza, Khudad Fort, Yaris and Dahran forts, Qubbat al-Zum, Marwaha Mosque, Bayn al-Dur Dome, Al-Jaami Mosque and more.
• Historic learning: Al-Zawahi School, Hujrat Wuhaza, and reading and book-copying evidence from Al-Mushriq.
• Biographies of scholars, jurists and linguists, with source and page references where available.
• Friday is listed for the weekly Hubaish market; confirm the current schedule locally.
• Search, categories, favorites, a local assistant and a knowledge quiz.
• Arabic, English, Turkish and French interfaces.
• Offline access to content after it has been loaded.

Source-aware documentation
Each record identifies its source and evidence status. Historical text is separated from local accounts. Subjects attributed to Al-Zawahi School remain provisional until checked against the printed reference. Sahban, al-Hubayshiyya and Dhu Hubaish are distinguished from present-day Hubaish boundaries.

This local knowledge project is not a substitute for government maps or specialist archaeological survey.
