Explore Hubaish District in Ibb Governorate, Yemen, through a digital guide to its history, geography, landmarks and local heritage.

Hubaish Smart Guide brings organized reference content about:
• Historic forts, castles and landmarks.
• Mosques and historic schools documented in available sources.
• Valleys, mountains, agricultural terraces and geographic places.
• Administrative sub-districts, villages and local profiles.
• Historical periods, notable figures and Yemeni references.

What you can do
• Browse the guide's history, landmarks and sub-district sections.
• Search for a place, sub-district or topic.
• Filter landmarks by category.
• Save places and records to your favorites.
• Revisit cached content when you are offline.
• Explore sourced heritage photographs when available.
• Test your knowledge of Hubaish history and geography.
• Use the app in Arabic, English, Turkish or French.

A source-aware local guide
The app connects entries to available historical and geographic references. It distinguishes documented information from local accounts and records that still need verification. Historical regions connected to Hubaish are kept separate from the district's current administrative boundaries. Records with unresolved geographic scope appear in their own section.

Designed for Hubaish residents, people from Ibb, students, researchers and anyone interested in Yemen's local history. Content can be expanded as reliable sources become available.

Hubaish Smart Guide is a community-focused reference project. It is not a replacement for official maps or specialist archaeological research.
